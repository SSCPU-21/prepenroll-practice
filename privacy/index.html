Privacy Notice

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This notice explains how PrepEnroll™ handles information submitted before accessing a Practice Studio.

What we collect

We collect the student name, curriculum, age group, and at least one contact method. For users under 18, the contact section is intended to be completed by a parent or legal guardian and we collect the parent/guardian name and at least one parent/guardian contact method. We also record the Practice Studio used, the date/time of submission, whether this notice was acknowledged, and whether optional marketing contact was selected.

Why we collect it

We use these details to provide access to the requested Practice Studio, maintain a record of the submission, respond to questions about the practice tool, protect the service from misuse, and—only where separately selected—send tutoring, academic-support or related updates.

Children and young people

If the student is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should complete the contact section. PrepEnroll™ does not use Practice Studio submissions for behavioural advertising or sell these details to advertisers.

Marketing choices

Marketing/tutoring updates are optional and are separate from access to the Practice Studio. You can ask us to stop these messages at any time.

Storage and service providers

Practice access submissions are stored using Supabase infrastructure on behalf of PrepEnroll™. Data may be processed in locations used by our service providers and may therefore be transferred across borders where legally permitted and protected by applicable safeguards.

Retention

Practice-access lead records are generally kept for up to 12 months unless you become an active PrepEnroll customer, ask us to delete the record sooner, or a longer period is required for legal or operational reasons.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent where consent is relied on, or other rights available under local data-protection law.

Privacy questions, access/deletion requests, or withdrawal of marketing consent:
info@prepenroll.com

Important

This notice is designed as a practical privacy baseline for an international educational website. Privacy requirements vary by country and may change; PrepEnroll™ may update this notice and its consent/age-verification process when required.
